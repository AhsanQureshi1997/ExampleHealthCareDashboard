This prototype is designed to support cross-setting care coordination by combining transitions-of-care signals, preventive care gaps, chronic disease quality indicators, and wearable recovery trends in a single workflow.
Prototype scope
Core functions shown here include patient-level risk stratification, shared task visibility, preventive care review using a USPSTF-style framework, chronic disease quality monitoring, and a simple wearables-informed recovery signal for outreach prioritization.
